### 2. Price-anchor ontology: five distinct things

1. `U_box`: old box's upper boundary — before breakout resistance; after accepted close a **potential** support.
2. `M_box`: negotiated center / `박스 중가` within a **multi-bar old box**, shown by the author's compression-to-candle pedagogy (#065/#070). It is not mechanically identical to `(U_box+L_box)/2` or the close of one real adjustment candle; derive from evidenced cluster of closes / trading activity on an actual chart.
3. `L_box`: old box's bottom boundary — one possible deeper historical structure anchor; not necessarily a good risk stop for a buy at `U_box` if distance is excessive.
4. `C_event`: high-participation *new breakout reference candle's close* (#077). Example `12,600` on **top of** old `U_box=12,000`, `M_box=11,300`. Newer event prices can be relevant without becoming the old box's middle by definition.
5. `H_adjust, C_adjust, L_adjust`: **actual subsequent adjustment candle** wick/high, close and low from user PDF U-002; formed after impulse, different from the hypothetical compressed many-bar box. Depending on chart, `C_adjust` might be above/below/near any old-box line; no right to silently collapse it with `M_box` or `C_event`.

This distinction is an **interpretive model to preserve source provenance**, not a demonstrated price forecast. Every plotted level should record `anchor_type`, `anchor_start/end`, `timeframe`, `known_asof`, `close_adjustment`, `evidence_strength` and `competing_interpretation`.
