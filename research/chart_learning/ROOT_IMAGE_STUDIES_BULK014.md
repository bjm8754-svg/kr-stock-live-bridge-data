## Visually resolved teaching cases

### BTC/KRW — apparent sloped 'support lines' are software indicators

- `1787696307713.jpeg` shows a BTC/KRW two-panel app screen with a daily interval selected, a quote 109,595,000 KRW and several thick colored lines. `1000056913.jpg` displays a near-time 109,610,000 KRW quote and a weekly interval selected, while `chart-1(4).png` is a crop from the **same weekly BTC chart**, not a third independent prediction.
- The **original indicator legend** in both captures labels `단순 MA 5 / 10 / 20 / 60 / 120 / 200`. Thick **red 60**, **gray 120**, and **green 200**-period MA curves are rendered by the platform; the thin/shorter MAs, Ichimoku, marked prior-day references and oscillator traces likewise are not verified discretionary analyst anchors. The thick green line may look like a drawn rising support ray when cropped, but its curve, legend and continuity across panels identify an automatic indicator.
- Important rival reading: if one chose a hand-drawn low-pivot trendline, its end points and ex-ante timestamp would have to be recorded separately. **No such manually placed line was proven in this cohort.** A later touch/near-touch of a lagging MA does not demonstrate that someone predicted support or obtained a fill. This matters directly to the user's request to reconstruct **why** a line was drawn rather than merely archiving line-looking photos.

- `1000056470.jpg`, `1000056468.jpg`, `1000056480.jpg`, `1000056477.jpg` (Aug10) and `1000056578.jpg`, `1000056589.jpg` (Aug13) are stock/ETF price screens with app-supplied colored bands, moving averages, time and oscillator panels. The source screenshots do **not** provide a prior fixed forecast, independent manual trendline object, completed-bar cutoff or recorded order fills.
- One stock/news context series (eight Aug10 finance forum/article screenshots plus an Aug07 macro calendar) supplies narrative and possible catalysts, **not** independently validated trading outcomes. Source timing and reliability must be checked before using claims as chart-method evidence.
