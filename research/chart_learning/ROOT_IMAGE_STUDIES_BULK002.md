## Hypotheses retained, NOT canonically promoted

1. **Price information time:** record `t_anchor` (when the reference price became knowable), `t_asof` (decision cutoff) and `t_order` (limit reservation), plus timeframe, intrabar wick, period close and adjustment; never infer buy fills using later screenshots. Compare shallow-limit/deep-limit/reclaim-confirmation/abstention with nonfill opportunity cost and finite-loss budget on a future truly unseen chart.
2. **Participation conditionality:** a high-volume breakout *can* be stopped by overhead supply, not proof of accumulator intent. Compare turnover with prior own-volume baseline, float and market regime; don't transfer source's 1,000억원 unconditionally.
3. **RSI is an auxiliary:** 70/30 and bullish divergence are conditional strength/weakness descriptions, not executable triggers; establish pivot availability as-of, same RSI smoothing/timeframe, and countertrend risk before testing.
4. **Source-authority humility:** derivative learning cards, teacher explanations and assistant screenshots are separate evidence classes. A 45-image research pass improves provenance and identifies sharper questions, **not measured AI trading skill, tested edge or investment profit**.
