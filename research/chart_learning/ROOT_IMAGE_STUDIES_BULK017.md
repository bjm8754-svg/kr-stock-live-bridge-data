2. **Korean and US mobile equity chart screens (Apr01/06/15/19/20/29).** Most are app-derived price/volume, MA, Bollinger, Ichimoku or automatic horizontal price-volume-bin overlays. In SKC 1000051846.jpg, the beige stacked horizontal bars are the platform's labelled **'매물대 수(10)'** price-volume ranges, not arbitrary blue-box drawing objects. The Kolon TissueGene Apr06 screen names MA5/20/60/120 directly; a sloping curve through nearby lows is not discretionary just because it visually touches candles. None has a separately proven line drawing object with before-cutoff pivot timestamps.

3. **FM Korea Apr29 1777430504614.jpeg.** Screenshot shows blue price trace above layered **red horizontal liquidity heatmap bands**, plus independent colored order-size group series ($100–$1k, $1k–$10k, $10k–$100k, etc.). The bands encode platform-observed/resting order-book liquidity, not an analyst hand-drawn horizontal supply line and **not necessarily executed volume at price**. Large visible bands might suggest *potential* market response areas; they can move/cancel before a test. Distinguish passive orders, executions, actual price progress, and source time alignment before drawing any absorption/CVD conclusion.
