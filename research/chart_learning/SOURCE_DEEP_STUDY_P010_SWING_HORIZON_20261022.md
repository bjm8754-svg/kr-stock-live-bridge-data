## Swing-chart analytical authority hierarchy (research draft, not a profitable algorithm)
1. **Market regime / weekly and monthly:** Multiweek direction and prior supply, event close, important range, sector/market risk and possible months-long trend. No forced bullish outlook on a weak market.
2. **Daily price-time-participation:** Decide whether genuine basing, breakout, reclaim or failed-acceptance state exists, where prior price-level roles change, and whether prior resistance offers realistic room to run. Establish support/resistance *before* the hypothetical subsequent contact.
3. **Plan and downside as one package:** Resting **first entry** at a case-justified level, an **optional second** that is permitted only under a precommitted still-valid structural thesis, actual invalidation (and separate emergency capital cap), relevant overhead objectives and review triggers. Tick size / gap / partial fills / expiry / budget matter. No forced equal-weight or 1:2 split.
4. **4h / 1h only for refinement:** Check local acceptance, distribution or a price reaction affecting daily entry quality; they may also warn about short-term whipsaw. Neither a 1h RSI crossing nor a completed 15m momentum divergence cancels a still-valid weekly thesis by itself.
5. **RSI / CVD divergence secondary:** Demand **same-timeframe, same-symbol, completed-pivot comparisons**. Confirm support/resistance and *price progress* first. CVD needs actually authenticated aggressor-trade series, units, venue and matching anchor/reset; without it classify **NOT VERIFIED**. Avoid calling an RSI fall during a higher daily close 'bearish divergence' without qualifying two swing highs.
6. **Swing management:** Record first entry nonfill vs first fill vs second fill; HOLD preserves actual shares, then ADD/REDUCE/EXIT only for explicit reasons. Event/catalyst, weekly structure, invalidation and emergency risk can trigger early exit. A scheduled 3-day check does **not** automatically liquidate or force a new stock. Unfinished positions at an experiment endpoint are open, not realized wins/losses.

## Three distinct chart outcomes to learn from (including an adverse case)
- **Continuation without pullback** (#067): a patient planned limit may remain unfilled. Cost is *missed opportunity*, not a stopped-out trade; cannot score a hypothetical later fill.
- **Shallow retest** (#073/#080): an old U defended with a completed-bar price response might justify a first entry under a locked swing thesis; later failure still possible.
- **Deep pullback/box return** (#073/#080): a second lower price is not automatically value. Falling through the prior structural reference can invalidate the original scenario, while increasing exposure raises gap/post-add loss. Never improve nominal RR by treating a tiny arbitrary stop as structural support.
