## 3. Interpretations kept separate

**A. Compressed-interval close:** #048/#065/#070 explicitly use this explanation. If a precise interval, venue, adjusted-price convention and final constituent bar are specified, its final close is in principle calculable. This is narrower than claiming that the entire supplied corpus uniquely selects that interval or that every blue line equals it. Moving the interval endpoint until a later retest “fits” would change the question after seeing the answer.

**B. Representative internal agreement price:** #047/#070/#071 also use agreement/importance language; #064/#068 mention a formation-candle close. These might be explanations of A, a separate reference convention, or editorial inconsistency. No VWAP/POC/current-inventory evidence resolves them. Do not pick one to hide the ambiguity.

**C. Arithmetic midpoint:** #047 rejects a simple midpoint, and #050 gives an explicit non-midpoint internal example. This is evidence against treating midpoint as a universal source rule. It does not identify the intended replacement formula.

**D. Ordinary closing-event predicate:** #066/#077/#079 use period close to judge a breakout/reentry or new event. This is a different role from naming the older blue internal anchor. Same Korean characters do not make them the same object.

The legacy aliases `C_box`, `M_box`, `C_reference`, `C_event`, `C_adjust` are indexes to prior research claims; **not measured variables merely because they have symbols**. Record exact source label and local role. Do not globally rename one into another, equate internal and adjustment references, or calculate an unlabeled value from a visually centered line.
