## Annotated channel Case A — actual visible structure, interpretation gated

- **Two manifestations, one source episode:** `1788851445435.jpeg` is a phone/gallery capture of a TradingView graph; `chart-1(8).png` is a clear crop of the same underlying chart. Count 2 files, **1 distinct chart**. It appears labeled Binance, 2h, but exact instrument string, price-unit semantics, timeframe and chart capture timestamp must be reconfirmed at native axis/text resolution before quantitative reconstruction.
- **Observed marks:** a broad **upward-sloping blue parallel channel** with an upper and lower solid outer rail, an interior solid line, and dotted interior parallels; about nine translucent purple rectangles marking swing extremes, contact/rejection or role-transition areas. The bottom solid rail visually coincides with a deep early-August trough; interior solid diagonal has touch/near-touch candidates at a late-May low, early-June low, early/mid-July crossing, August advance high and early-September approach. Upper rail visually references a late-May/early-June top zone.
- **Hypothesis H-A1 (parallel swing geometry):** an analyst selected an initial pair of swing lows or highs to set slope, then duplicated/offset the rail through a separate extreme. Purple boxes could indicate reaction clusters, not necessarily the two exact anchor clicks. Test body-vs-wick endpoints, whether 2+ same-side touches precede the evaluation cutoff, and whether relocating the line by one candle changes reactions.
- **Competing H-A2 (retrospective fitting):** the full visible range includes the July drop below the interior rail and a deep August trough; rails and boxes may have been adjusted after those events. The later September approach to the sloping interior line is **not ex-ante validation**. A visually impressive touched line can be one of many parallel offsets.
- **Counterexample/invalidation to inspect:** the July transition from interior-rail support to violation, August pivot at the lower rail, and September return to the formerly breached interior area may be structurally different events. Distinguish closed-candle hold/reclaim, rejection, wick-only crossings and whether price reacted **after** a line was specified, not before. The chart contains no time-stamped prior annotation state, order, fill, exit or performance record.
- **Practical annotation reconstruction test:** (1) fix instrument/venue and 2h cutoff; (2) expose only bars before late July, fit rails from available swing clusters without seeing August/September; (3) freeze coordinates/scale; (4) reveal withheld bars and record touch, overshoot, failed role flip and channel redraw required; (5) compare against horizontal swing zones / market structure baseline and alternative body-anchored lines. Until then mark **ANNOTATION_HYPOTHESIS, NOT METHOD PROMOTION**.
