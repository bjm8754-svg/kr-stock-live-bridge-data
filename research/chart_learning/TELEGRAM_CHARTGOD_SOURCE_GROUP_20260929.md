- **Multitimeframe reaction:** Intraday 15m/1h divergence, 50-region retests and daily trend must be read together; 1h momentum can remain strong while a different timeframe rejects. The group has useful *case questions*, not confirmed exact indicator resets or venue-qualified prices.
- **Trade execution / non-chasing / timing:** Several fragments discuss resisting impulsive entries and adapting short-term decisions, but contain other participants' questions and possible retrospective anecdote. No attributable complete trade ledger, fill, fee, risk, stop or unbiased universe.
