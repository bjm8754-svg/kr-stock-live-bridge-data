### Exact price anchoring terms must stay distinct
- `C_reference`: historical **single** event/reference candle close; requires precise candle ID and adjustment state.
- `C_event`: newly generated **breakout** event candle close; may later be a separate new price anchor.
- `H_adjust/C_adjust/L_adjust`: one **actual** adjustment bar's OHLC from U-002 lecture concept.
- `U_box/C_box/L_box`: a bounded multi-bar teaching structure; **C_box is a conceptual internal close, no proven formula**. Don't substitute `(U+L)/2`, last ordinary bar close, VWAP or Volume Profile POC.
- `connector`: prior structural pivot/resumption candle close **chosen and frozen before** a future retest; if chosen afterwards it is hindsight.
- `sloped level`: two or more genuine prior pivot anchors, verified scale and source edit time; unlike horizontal levels, endpoint uncertainty accumulates along extrapolated x-axis.

## C. Distinct decision clocks that corpus does not resolve into one recipe

1. **Old candidate discovery clock**: old significant price exists before future revisit (source cutoff), not retroactively after successful retest.
2. **Event clock**: underthrow, intrabar resistance crossing, gap, adjustment candle, new event volume, RSI divergence **each has different qualifying evidence**.
3. **Settlement clock**: fully finished 1h/daily/weekly/monthly close determines structural role; an unfinished candle must not claim settled support. Source #036 precisely distinguishes lower wick versus close below.
4. **Reaction clock**: after *later* revisit, the price either holds, rejects, or persists against previous interpretation. A later confirmation cannot justify an earlier pre-touch low-price fill retrospectively.
5. **Capital-risk clock**: source weekly/monthly close-based structure does not guarantee executable stop or loss bounds during gap, illiquid event or intra-month drawdown.
6. **Authorship clock**: a successful replay screenshot can show a hand-drawn line, but unless edit/drawing history or pre-registered chart exists, no proven pre-event placement.
