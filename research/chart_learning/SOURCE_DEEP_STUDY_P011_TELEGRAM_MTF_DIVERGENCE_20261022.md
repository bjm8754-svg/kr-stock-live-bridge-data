## Distinguish *type* versus *timeframe*
- **Regular bullish:** price lower swing low + RSI higher at corresponding low; **regular bearish:** price higher swing high + RSI lower at corresponding high.
- **Hidden bullish:** price higher swing low + RSI lower at corresponding low; **hidden bearish:** price lower swing high + RSI higher at corresponding high.
- These mathematical definitions do not tell which timeframe dominates or whether a particular pivot is known **at** the pivot. Fix swing/pivot confirmation delays, completed bars, and RSI settings *before* looking ahead. Hidden divergence is an analytical taxonomy, not something directly proven by the reviewed teacher screenshots.
- **CVD divergences are NOT RSI divergences:** require trade-side classified aggressor flow, instrument/venue, volume units, reset/anchor and timestamp-matched price progression. **No reviewed screenshot has verified raw CVD series.** Do not infer passive absorption from RSI alone.
