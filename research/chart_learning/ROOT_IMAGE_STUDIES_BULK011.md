## User line-annotation priority

Preserve separately: (A) hand-drawn analyst trendline/horizontal/box/Fib with reconstructable anchor, (B) automatic indicator line (MA/Ichimoku/volume bands), (C) tutorial arrows pointing at interface controls, and (D) screenshot cropping artifacts. In the present cohorts, **no personally drawn price-anchor line was verified with high confidence**; the chart overlays are mostly B and the Sep01 red arrows are C. Do not invent an author's trendline rationale.

**Hypothesis for later validation:** 600/1000 MA may serve as context for long-base/trend recovery but must be compared with price structure, prior closes, supply, timeframe and false signals. Mere existence of the indicator/color setting is not a tested entry rule. Compare the teacher's original KakaoTalk text and curated 91 source on exact anchor/line semantics.
