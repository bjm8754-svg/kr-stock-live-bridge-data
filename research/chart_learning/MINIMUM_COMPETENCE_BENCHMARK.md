## Acceptance rule

**The minimum has NOT been demonstrated yet.** A future review can label 'MINIMUM DEMONSTRATED' only when **all** are satisfied:

1. Multiple independently locked, **full-universe point-in-time** evaluations include both good and adverse market regimes. Researcher and evaluator have neither future prices nor knowledge of future-selected stock lists at decision time.
2. The review produces defensible, conditional trade plans of quality plausibly **at least comparable to the five expert example plans**, evaluated *blind* on structural reasoning and risk rather than closeness to the expert's numbers. Evaluation criteria and graders must be fixed **before** viewing candidate outcomes.
3. A sufficiently broad forward SHADOW track record shows **realistically implementable**, cost-aware decision quality and economic benefit over transparent baseline(s), not just paper targets reached. Report uncertainty, sample size, nonfill rate, cash drag, max drawdown and selection bias.
4. Subsequent sessions recover the cumulative research state and **do not regress on known corrected failures**, as demonstrated by fixed regression cases. This is a process condition; model weights are not being retrained by GitHub files.

No arbitrary numerical profit/win-rate threshold is asserted from five example plans. Quantitative pass/fail thresholds need a **separately precommitted evaluation design** and credible sample size. No single analysis, profitable trade, or GitHub CI document PASS can satisfy this research goal.
