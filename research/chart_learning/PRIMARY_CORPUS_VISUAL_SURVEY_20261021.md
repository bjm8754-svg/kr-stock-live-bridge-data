## SRC-STUDY-001 reconciliation: separate all three entry plans at a fixed as-of cutoff

1. **Preemptive support-zone limit (L0):** Candidate only when support area existed **before the order** and the support-to-invalidation distance plus market/gap risk is justified. Lower nominal entry may fill into an actual failed box. Distinct from a hypothetical later spring condition.
2. **Spring reclaim conditional (L1):** Must observe a real attempt to persist **below** the lower boundary and a return **into** the accepted range. It sacrifices early fill and can miss a fast rally; a subsequent second breakdown is a genuine invalidation scenario.
3. **Confirmed upper-bound/center role test (L2):** An earlier resistance must first be overcome on the *relevant completed timeframe*, then defended; meaningful pullback can stop near the upper region or deepen toward the center. An upper-only resting limit can miss an advancing market; a center-only resting limit can fill in a broken structure or never fill. No one level is categorically superior.
4. **Invalidation vs price action:** Record `C_reference` (pre-existing anchor close), `C_adjust` (later qualified change in accepted price), `H_adjust` (overhead rejection), `L_adjust` (downside failed-break extremum), `M_box` (hypothesized box consensus). Do not invent any numeric values where original bars, price-volume profile, drawing timestamps or market venue are missing.
5. **Opportunity costs:** Record filled trades, unfilled pullback orders, market rallies missed, time in cash, false break losses, gap/slippage, fees, partial fills and benchmark under the *same* prior-fixed rules. Future-informed replay visuals cannot be graded.
