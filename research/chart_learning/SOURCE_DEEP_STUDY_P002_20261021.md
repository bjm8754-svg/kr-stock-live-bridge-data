## 2. Core ontology conflict: single adjustment candle vs compressed multi-candle box

The 14-page **edited speech transcript**, its opening overview and ABL바이오/SK하이닉스 passages, teaches **one identifiable `조정봉` as a real candle**:
- `H_adjust` = that candle's high, source of potential overhead selling encounter
- `L_adjust` = that candle's low, source of potential buying response
- `C_adjust` = **the actual exchange-sourced close of that candle**, described as the '심판' / temporary decision price; its bar timeframe (1D/1W/1M) matters
- Must record the source bar's **date, adjustment/corporate-action basis, settled vs developing state**; no independent verified video chart/candle dataset is supplied in PDF.

Original #030/#031/#070 instead introduce **a range of multiple candles** then a **synthetically compressed `U_box/C_box/L_box`** structure. The pictures label `C_box` in a blue internal line as important, but **provide NO unique OHLCV construction** of that compressed close. Actual multi-day close, last-day close, midpoint, time-weighted volume cost, and volume-by-price POC are **not source-equivalent by default**.

Therefore the common shortcut **`C_adjust = C_box`** is invalid unless an actual chart/source establishes coincidence, even if both use the Korean word '종가'. A user-programmer cannot directly code the latter as a simple OHLCV formula from the source material.

`C_reference` of the high-participation important prior candle (#029 or lecture TXT) is a third distinct datum; the new breakout event `C_event` may be a fourth. They require separate source IDs and knowledge timestamps.

## 3. Structural retest dispute: is a retest compulsory?

**The direct contradiction matters more than superficial agreement:**

- The edited lecture at SK하이닉스 and other examples repeatedly argues that an upper-box breakout **'와야 된다' / should retest** old support, often using already completed replay outcomes to demonstrate a later touch. It admits an example of a planned retest **not filling before price ran upward**.
- **Actual numbered #067** includes in the first left panel both **straight continuation with no retest** and **a later retest pathway**, explicitly warns that chasing immediately after breakout can be risky, and says waiting for a pullback can forfeit entry if none occurs.
- **#069** defines U role flip and suggests *observe response at retest*, not that price cannot go up until retesting.
- **#071/#072** present U shallow defense and C_box deeper defense as different *conditional* support tests.
- **#073** gives an educational staged buy example **12,000 first /11,300 second /13,500 later price**, with **50%+50%** labels expressly marked '비중은 예시입니다' (weight percentages are examples). A 50/50 default strategy is **not** in the source as a validated optimum.

**Resolution:** Frame 'a retest after a breakout' as a **preferred way to get risk-defined entry at previously meaningful prices**, not a market law. Three possible paths must remain distinct: immediate continuation (no fill for waiting limits), shallow U retest, deeper C_box retest that may turn into breakdown. A 'must retest' forecast in a retrospective lecture cannot become a guaranteed future event. Post-event successful retests are strongly prone to educational selection.

## 4. Critical risk dispute — monthly/weekly close vs earlier emergency drawdown

The edited lecture explicitly uses **ABL바이오** monthly adjustment, including reported monthly downside around **20% within the month** before the month end and a discussion of waiting until the last calendar/trading session before a structural close violation. The lecturer then describes **sell now versus wait for breakeven rebound** alternatives when the prior line has failed. These statements are source narratives, **not an insured fill or evidence the breakeven bounce will happen**.

The properly separate reasoning clocks:
1. **Thesis clock:** For a monthly structural line, the qualified **monthly completed close** determines whether the thesis is invalidated. For a weekly line, the qualified weekly completed close; daily line, daily close.
2. **Capital clock:** Maximum money/portfolio loss, unusual corporate event risk, exchange limit moves, gaps and execution liquidity **still bind intraperiod**. A monthly close requirement cannot justify unlimited -20%, -30% drawdown while waiting for a distant monthly settlement.
3. **Post-invalidation recovery is a NEW hypothesis:** A future rebound to breakeven is optional and uncertain. Averaging down after documented structural invalidation cannot be silently retrofitted as original plan success.
4. **Position sizes and fills:** The speaker's retrospective examples of split entry, more size at deeper close, or escape during rebound **do not define universally sustainable sizing or prove actual historic fills**. The PDF contains speaker claims and replay-like recollections; independent original video/OHLCV/fill records are absent.
