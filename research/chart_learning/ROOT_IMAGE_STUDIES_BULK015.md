- The two late captures show unusually **thick sweeping blue and yellow freeform curves**, a **near-horizontal green stroke around the 3,250,000 zone**, and a thick lower red stroke. Unlike fine app MA5/10/20/60/120/200 indicator traces visibly named in the legend, these *appear to be editorial/manual highlights*. **Candidate manual markup — NOT VERIFIED author, creation timestamp, exact anchor rule or pre-candle availability.** The upper stroke might denote prior high/supply, a rounded-top arc, or arbitrary illustration; the lower red stroke could mark dynamic support rather than an actual multi-pivot trendline. Do not relabel a line as deliberate support from how subsequent candles behaved.
- Candidate measurement checklist on the source: identify before-cutoff pivot bars and body/wick selection, distinguish 3,250,000 horizontal ceiling from indicator MA proximity, define acceptance as repeated closes/volume/time above, define failed retest as closes back into the prior box, and pre-register invalidation.
