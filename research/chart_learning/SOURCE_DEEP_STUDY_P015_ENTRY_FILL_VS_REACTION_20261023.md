## Critical logical separation: price location, information clock, order fill

| Intended entry | Information available BEFORE triggering | What the touch proves | What it does NOT prove | Failure / opportunity cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Resting BUY_LIMIT near premarked U** | Historical U and completed close beyond U, but no knowledge of future retest response | If market reaches the order under a stated fill model, it *may* fill | U has been accepted as support on the same touch; a subsequent closing rejection can still occur | Failure may produce loss; no-retest continuation leaves the order unfilled and forfeits participation |
| **B. Resting BUY_LIMIT near premarked C_box** | Exact C_box must first be defined from cutoff-visible data; U breakout known | Touch reaches a deeper old-box area and *may* fill | A bullish reaction at C_box or continued structural validity | Falling through C_box after fill can invalidate; risk distance and gap must be costed, not characterized as guaranteed better value |
| **C. Confirmed-reaction entry** | Need a *completed*, pre-specified rejection/reclaim bar after U/C_box test | A reaction has become observable at its close | Entry at original pre-reaction limit price or a guaranteed next-bar fill | Higher paid price, possible no-fill after jump; different trade than A/B, cannot take both their favorable price and its later information |
| **D. Continue without retest** | Confirmed breakout; no later touch | Breakout persists so far | A fill in A/B, or a verified economical chase | Opportunity cost to limits, uncertain continuation risk for chase |

**Proposed source-based, NOT yet empirically validated classification:** `PENDING_BREAKOUT` (intrabar U penetration) → `CLOSE_ABOVE_U` (qualified bar close) → one of `NO_RETEST`, `U_TOUCH_UNRESOLVED`, `U_DEFENDED`, `DEEP_C_TOUCH_UNRESOLVED`, `C_DEFENDED`, `FAILED_ACCEPTANCE`. Assign `U_DEFENDED`/`C_DEFENDED` only after a *specified* completed response, not at the price touch; do not infer the original limit order would fill at the later confirmed price. A true price below C_box and completed close failure must be assessed with the separately defined emergency capital cap and the correct higher-timeframe structural rules, never with a made-up 1-tick stop.

## Discrepancy deliberately NOT resolved

The illustrations teach both **resting levels** (#073) and **response confirmation** (#074/#076/#080), but they do not specify the order type/timing, bar completion, fill/slippage, or exact C_box derivation that would reconcile both into ONE executable strategy. Replacing that missing information with an assumed post-touch perfectly priced entry is hindsight bias. Distinguish **standing limit before reaction** from **signal after reaction** until independently observed market data can compare them under a fixed cutoff. The phrase `C_box` is source ontology, not numerically calculable yet.

## Contradictions / adverse cases to preserve

1. U holds but never returns to U: limit trader may miss the rally (#067).
2. U touches and briefly bounces, then day closes back in old box (#078): touch or one intrabar green candle is not durable acceptance.
3. C_box limit fills first, subsequent completed bars break and remain below C_box (#076): deepest apparent bargain is a thesis failure, not mandatory average-down.
4. Post-event C_event=12,600 is visible at close only (#077): annotating this as a pre-breakout available historical support is temporal leakage.
5. High volume on a break followed by persistent box reentry (#075/#078): participation cannot substitute for subsequent price acceptance.
