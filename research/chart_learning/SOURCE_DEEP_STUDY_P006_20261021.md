Tall late-stage activity bars coexist with marked support candidates. **Three cropped images are one editorial source**, not independent predictions.
- **Possible rational line ancestry:** descending upper rail appears to pass through older peaks; lower black rail roughly tracks old lower turning zones; blue bands cover multiple preceding lateral price consolidations; right-side black ovals near pullback basing and former resistance areas flag probable reaction locations.
- **What is unverified:** independent original TradingView/broker OHLC, which highs/lows vs close were chosen as exact anchors, whether the author fixed the diagonals before the spring 2026 rally, drawing edit timestamps, indicators' data adjustments, and separate line touches at a matching fully completed candle. Also some chart axes are **normalized percent-relative or screen-scaled**; do not translate screen-y into exact trading prices without axes.
- **Poster's numerical thesis:** social text says around **14,000 KRW** is important and suggests potential **'50%' gains**. These are **source-author claims**. They are *not* a verified structural invalidation level, loss-capped order, independent target forecast or performance. A 14k breach, failure to sustain prior highlighted boxes, or rejection at the outer descending rail are independent adverse paths requiring actual historical dated close evidence.
- **Time caution:** file upload cohort May31 versus displayed chart headers May26/May29 are distinct, and displayed price 16,090 is a quote on the archived screen. One cannot infer what the same drawn lines looked like before May26 or assign past entries from the May31 post.
- **Prior study comparison:** educational #032 event-close method and #033 long-range box have distinct theoretical sources; the LG illustration combines long-term diagonal, intermediate horizontal zones, and relatively recent high volume, none of which automatically override the other. ## New METHOD QUESTIONS (NOT new algorithmic rules)
1. **Level reduction:** Which of LG's several overlapping blue boxes, long sloped rails and short chart ovals remains informative when the timeframe is selected before outcome? A screenshot with too many marks can make every price touch look meaningful.
2. **Fixed data language:** A level must include `instrument, venue, timeframe, completed_at, anchor_kind, value_or_range, mark_author, created_at_confidence, source_filename, source_snapshot_time`; most social screenshots omit `created_at` and `completed_at`.
3. **Same-chart paired snapshots vs independent confirmations:** LG three images = one article; ETH Apr03 duplicate = one screen; ZM social thesis and platform chart = one comparable underlying instrument, **not two profitable signals**.
4. **Manual mark disambiguation:** Until true hand-drawn indicator identities and editing metadata are available, don't impose slope-fit rules on moving averages, crosshair rectangles or UI arrows.
5. **Alternative survival:** Strong opening above $90, on-screen 14k claimant, or ETH 3.25m line crossing without qualified close and later reactions is **setup context**, not support adoption. Watch/no-trade is a legitimate conclusion in eventual tests.
