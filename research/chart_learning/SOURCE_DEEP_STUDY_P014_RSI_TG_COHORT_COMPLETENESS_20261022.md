## Incremental methodological corrections before continuing other Library sources

1. **Source-group taxonomy:** A 39-file upload cohort ≠ 39 source-authored charts. Record per-file `IMAGE_TYPE`, `SPEAKER_CONFIDENCE`, `PRICE_TIMEFRAME`, `INDICATOR_EXPLICIT`, `MARK_CREATED_AT`, `QUALIFIED_TWO_PIVOTS`; remove duplicate screenshots and non-chart image objects from actual technical chart study.
2. **RSI annotation hierarchy:** A hand-drawn line on the **RSI pane** is not a hand-drawn price trendline. To call divergence, identify two same-instrument, same-timeframe **price** pivots **and their corresponding RSI levels** at the *price bar* endpoints. An RSI slope drawn between convenient indicator peaks that do not correspond to price pivots is not sufficient.
3. **Swing hierarchy remains:** Weekly/monthly major structure and supply → completed daily entry thesis/risk/space → 4h/1h local observation → 15m optional execution only. If hourly RSI divergence is contrary to a weakened weekly structure, WATCH or REJECT long, not an automatic multiweek buy.
4. **As-of rule:** Source screenshot upload time is not author mark timestamp, indicator alert time, or exchange candle settlement. Don't award accuracy from historic highlights or self-reported performance.
