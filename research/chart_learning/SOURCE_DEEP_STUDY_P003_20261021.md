## 2. Spring as successive *states*, not a forward reversal prediction

- #053 depicts synthetic range floor **10,000**, a down-wick **9,800** and subsequent **10,100** return; the relevant observation is inability to sustain prices below the old lower boundary. Does **not** prove actual opponent intent or next-day momentum.
- #054 explicitly denies spring = **guaranteed uptrend**; it is evidence that an attempted downside exit **has so far failed**.
- #055 shows why a **second qualified bar** can add information: early undercut/recovery must be temporally distinguished. Whether the source means one or two bars in a particular case cannot be inferred from the cartoon's red/green bodies alone without first defining the cutoff.
- #057 states **bottom arrival = location only**; **underthrow + re-entry = more path information**; uses more precise phrase *'failed downside attempt'* rather than *'support permanently verified'*.
- #059 explicitly traces **L→C_box→U** as resistance/reclaim tests after spring; source graph also depicts an alternative **failure at C_box and renewed fall**. Therefore spring, inner-range progress and upper-range acceptance are separate propositions.

**Non-negotiable temporal rule:** A hypothetical prior buy resting at 9,800 cannot be retrospectively claimed to have been motivated by a spring at 10,100 **unless that was a separate, already pre-registered order thesis**. Price advantage and information certainty are traded against one another. No higher timeframe completion assumption from educational diagrams.

## 5. Learning contradictions, candidate protocol repairs, and why not code yet

1. **Level source class determines legitimate construction:** completed historical event close `C_reference` vs actual adjustment candle `H_adjust/C_adjust/L_adjust` vs bounded box `U_box/C_box/L_box` vs multi-point sloping ray. Passing all to a single `level` variable obscures origin and timeframe.
2. **Bargain price is not success evidence:** cartoon #050 has a nominal 100 downside, but real close-time stop plus spreads/gaps can exceed it. Without distribution of follow-through and fills, a high notional reward ratio is not an edge.
3. **Spring return vs probable bullish future:** #053–#059 merely add information that downside was rejected *so far*, followed by tests of inner C_box and upper U. Calling spring after the underthrow without the return creates lookahead.
4. **Historical highlights can be post hoc:** PENDLE/ALTEO/JEONJIN pairs and hand-marked channels cannot claim pre-cutoff predictive performance. A marked line is an interpretable object whose chronology must be established.
5. **Curated teaching drawings and real charts are different source tiers:** #046–#065 are cartoon explanations; #086–#091 are price screenshots with unknown drawing chronology/adjustment; neither currently provides authenticated full tradable price history or fills.
