## 2. Moving ray, horizontal price and shaded zone are different objects
A descending line represents a reference at a particular time coordinate. Its price can change as time advances; a fixed reservation is a fixed price. The 1,188,000 callout at its own anchor is therefore not automatically a fixed future buy limit. Without axis mode, exact time anchors and intended decision time, no executable projection is certified.

Candidate source-reading record:
- HORIZONTAL_LEVEL: one stated price/zone, with origin bar and time;
- PROJECTED_LINE: original anchor points, chart coordinate mode, and value **at the intended evaluation time**;
- GEOMETRIC_DERIVATIVE: parent anchors plus declared subdivision rule;
- AREA: explicit upper/lower bounds and whether they move with time.

This is a **candidate taxonomy**, not a new trading algorithm. Do not silently convert line(t) into a standing constant-price order or continuously amend hypothetical reservations. Order price/timing, structural invalidation and emergency money risk must be specified separately in a future approved test.

## 6. Candidate method refinement and falsification conditions
Before assigning weight to an annotated price:
1. classify **how the line was generated**, including whether it shares parent anchors with other lines;
2. identify instrument/venue/as-of/bar interval/session and whether the bar is complete;
3. decide what state is actually observed: location, live penetration, completed close, later defense, or failure;
4. separate the hypothesis from any future order design and money-risk limit;
5. retain the strongest opposite reading.

Use geometric children and repeated timeframe views as **dependent context**, not votes. Do not discard grids merely for being geometric; do not promote them merely because many historical turns touch them.

Falsifiers to retain for later approved validation: grid fails with anchors frozen before a new path; reasonable alternate endpoints change line-side judgment; coarse grid performs like dense grid; current breakout closes back below reference; lower-frame reclaim coexists with higher-frame supply; advance limit fills and subsequent defense fails; no-retest rise leaves reservation unfilled. These are research designs, **not experiments executed here**.
